## About Me

Hi! I am Kunhan Gao. I’m pursuing a two-year M.S. in Computer Science at Yale University, advised by Prof. [Rex Ying](https://www.cs.yale.edu/homes/ying-rex/). Before joining Yale, I received my B.S. in Data Science from City University of Hong Kong.

My research interests lie in multimodal learning and long-context modeling. I have a growing interest in agent memory and knowledge systems, long-horizon reasoning, and agent reliability. I hope to contribute to the development of more capable, reliable, and general AI systems.
