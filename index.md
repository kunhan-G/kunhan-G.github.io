## About Me

I am Kunhan Gao, a thesis-track M.S. student in Computer Science at Yale University, advised by Prof. [Rex Ying](https://www.cs.yale.edu/homes/ying-rex/). I received my B.S. in Data Science from City University of Hong Kong.

## Research Interests

My research interests lie in multimodal learning and long-context modeling, and I have a growing interest in agent memory and knowledge systems, long-horizon reasoning, and agent reliability. I hope to contribute to the development of more capable, reliable, and general AI systems.
