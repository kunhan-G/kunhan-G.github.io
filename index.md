## About Me

I am Kunhan Gao, a thesis-track M.S. student in Computer Science at Yale University, advised by Prof. [Rex Ying](https://www.cs.yale.edu/homes/ying-rex/). I received my B.S. in Data Science from City University of Hong Kong.

## Research Interests

I am interested in multimodal learning and models that can use a long context. I am also interested in what an agent should remember, how it reasons across a long horizon, and how to keep that behavior reliable.
